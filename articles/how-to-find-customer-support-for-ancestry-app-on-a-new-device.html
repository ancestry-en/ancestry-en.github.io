How to Get Started

  1. Identify the exact account, billing, DNA, or family tree issue and note any error messages.
  2. Use Ancestry’s official help center or the support contact options shown in your signed in account.
  3. Check available contact channels and hours for your region.
  4. Never share passwords, verification codes, or full payment details with an unverified contact.

Before You Begin

Make sure you are using an authorized Ancestry account, a supported device, and a secure connection. Menu names and available features may differ by subscription, device, location, and current app version. Keep your email access available in case verification is required.

Common Problems and Troubleshooting

If Ancestry App does not behave as expected, first check connectivity, update your browser or app, and verify that you are using the correct account. A missing feature can also be caused by account permissions, subscription status, or an outdated app. Avoid repeatedly entering credentials into unfamiliar websites.

For family tree and DNA features, take extra care with privacy. Review sharing settings, keep downloaded files in protected storage, and avoid sending sensitive family information or raw DNA data to unknown third parties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I complete this on a phone?

Many Ancestry tasks work in a mobile browser or app, but some settings and downloads may require the website on a computer.

What if I cannot access my account?

Use the official account recovery flow, check your email access, and follow the identity verification prompts.

Will I lose my family tree data?

Routine sign in and app updates should not delete cloud stored records. Before deleting accounts, records, or files, check the consequences and keep appropriate backups.

Conclusion

Use trusted Ancestry account and app controls, confirm changes before proceeding, and protect personal and genetic data throughout the process.
